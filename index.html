ARTICLE I

Buy-In & Commitment

Annual Fee
$10 per manager, per year
Billing
Paid two years at a time ($20 due at the start of every two-year cycle)

If a manager wants to leave the league but has traded away future draft picks, they must still pay the buy-in for every season those picks cover — even after their two-year commitment ends.

Example: You traded your 2027 and 2028 first-round picks, then decide to leave after your first two years are up. You still owe the $10 buy-in for both 2027 and 2028, since another manager is holding your equity in those drafts.
ARTICLE II

The Draft

The off-season draft runs in order of finish from the prior regular season, worst record to best. The last-place team picks first; the top regular-season finisher picks last.

Tanking is, as always, a legitimate strategy.

ARTICLE IV

Weekly Scoring — Lock-In

We use Sleeper's default Lock-In format, not daily lineups. Each starter gets exactly one game per week counted toward your total — you choose which one.

If Jokić drops 50 on Tuesday, lock it in and his remaining games that week no longer affect your score. If Tuesday's a dud, skip it and wait for a better outing later in the week.

ARTICLE VII

Trade Deadline

Trading closes one week before playoffs begin. Every deal must be final before that cutoff.

ARTICLE VIII

Current Format
