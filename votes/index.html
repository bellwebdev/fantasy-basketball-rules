CATEGORY I

Roster & Taxi

01

Expand bench and taxi capacity

Pending vote

Add one or two bench spots plus another taxi spot, so rosters can absorb injuries and hold more young players.

Current

6 bench · 4 taxi

Proposed

7–8 bench · 5 taxi

The exact number of bench spots (one more or two) still needs to be pinned down before it goes to a vote.

Amends Article III

02

Mid-season taxi window

Pending vote

Open a short window during the season to move eligible players in and out of the taxi squad, so an injury doesn't strand a roster spot for the rest of the year.

Current

Taxi assignments lock before the season and stay locked

Proposed

One limited in-season window to reassign taxi players

CATEGORY II

Draft & Anti-Tanking

03

Seed the draft on max possible points

Pending vote

Base draft seeding on each team's maximum possible points (the best game each starter could have locked) instead of the points actually locked in. Deliberately locking bad games late in the year then does nothing for your pick.

Current

Seeding follows total points locked in

Proposed

Seeding follows max possible points

Filed under "I promise this happened."

Amends Article II

04

Regular-season record sets draft order

Pending vote

Draft order should come off the regular-season standings alone, with playoff results excluded. As it stands, a team knocked out early has every reason to stop trying.

Current

Final standings, with playoff finishes factored in

Proposed

Regular-season record only

This one reads more like a clarification than a change. Article II already says "prior regular season." Worth confirming what Sleeper is actually sorting on before we spend a vote on it.

Amends Article II

CATEGORY IV

Playoffs & Schedule

06

Cut the playoff field to six

Pending vote

Six teams make the playoffs instead of eight, so a postseason berth actually has to be earned.

Current

Top 8 overall, no divisions

Proposed

Top 6 overall

A six-team bracket needs first-round byes for the top two seeds. That should be settled in the same vote, and it ties directly into proposal 07.

Amends Article VIII

07

Rework the schedule

Pending vote

Extend the regular season by a week and rebuild the playoff calendar so no round lands on a short week.

Current

Regular season runs into a shortened playoff week

Proposed

One extra regular-season week, full-length playoff rounds

The trade deadline sits one week before playoffs begin, so it moves with the calendar automatically.

Amends Article VIII and Article VII

CATEGORY V

League Business

08

Raise the buy-in to $50

Pending vote

Move the annual buy-in from $10 to $50 to put real money behind the season.

Current

$10 per year · $20 per two-year cycle

Proposed

$50 per year

Two knock-on effects to decide: whether the two-year prepay stays (that's $100 up front), and whether the traded-pick obligation scales with it. At $50 a year, leaving with two future firsts traded away costs $100 on the way out.

Amends Article I

09

Waivers clear more than once a week

Pending vote

Add a second weekly waiver run so managers can react to injuries and rest days instead of eating a zero.

Current

FAAB clears every Tuesday

Proposed

Two clearing days per week, at minimum

The doc says "biweekly," which cuts both ways: twice a week or every other week. The stated reason (emergency injury and rest-day pickups) only works as twice a week, so that's how it's written here. Worth confirming with whoever proposed it.

Amends Article VI

10

Last-place punishment

Pending vote

Introduce a punishment for finishing last, or for putting up the lowest point total on the season.

Current

No consequence beyond the first pick

Proposed

A punishment, to be chosen by a separate vote

Two votes, really: whether we do this at all, and then what the punishment is. Also needs a trigger, since worst record and lowest total points aren't always the same team.
